# Pinterest Content Strategy - Phase 4 Growth & Monetization

## Overview
Launch 30 Pinterest pins across 4 content pillars to drive traffic to InteriorOfAI.com, 
with focus on US home products and design inspiration.

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Design Inspiration (8 pins)
- Before/after room transformations
- Living room style guides
- Bedroom sanctuary ideas
- Kitchen transformation concepts
- Color palette inspiration

### Pillar 2: Product Recommendations (10 pins)
- Furniture recommendations (living room, bedroom)
- Lighting ideas and solutions
- Rug and decor inspiration
- Shopping guides and roundups

### Pillar 3: AI Design Tips (6 pins)
- "How to use AI for interior design" tutorials
- Room styling tips
- Small space solutions
- Color palette guides

### Pillar 4: Brand/Community (6 pins)
- User results and testimonials
- AI tool showcase
- Community projects
- Behind-the-scenes

## Pin Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 2:3 (1000 x 1500 px)
- **File Size**: Under 2MB
- **File Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **Images**: Use 400x600px preview + 1000x1500px full size

## Metrics to Track
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Impressions | 50K-100K |
| Clicks | 500-1,000 |
| Saves | 500+ |
| Outbound Clicks | 100+ |
| Conversions | 10+ |

## Pinterest Optimization
- **Titles**: 80-100 characters, include keywords
- **Descriptions**: 200-300 characters, include CTA
- **Categories**: Home & Garden (interior design)
- **Board Organization**: 
  - "AI Interior Design Ideas"
  - "Home Decor Inspiration"
  - "Furniture Recommendations"
  - "Small Space Solutions"
